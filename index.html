About
Our Story

Founded with Grace. Built with Purpose. 22 Years of Excellence in Braj.

Baldeo Public School was established with a singular vision — to create a place of learning that honours the cultural richness of Braj while preparing students for a rapidly changing world. Over two decades, we have grown from a modest beginning into a thriving institution trusted by thousands of families across Mathura and beyond.

Our curriculum blends rigorous CBSE academics with co-curricular depth — sports, arts, leadership, and character development — because we believe a complete education shapes not just careers, but lives.

Primary & Secondary

A Foundation That
Lasts a Lifetime

From Play Group through Class X, our primary and secondary programme emphasises conceptual clarity, mother-tongue literacy, and strong numeracy — the building blocks every student needs for success at senior secondary level and beyond.

  • Play Group / Nursery / KG — joyful, activity-based learning
  • Classes I–V — NCERT-aligned with project-based methods
  • Classes VI–X — CBSE Board preparation with practicals
  • Dedicated Science, Computer, and Language labs
  • Regular PTMs, progress reports, and individual mentoring
